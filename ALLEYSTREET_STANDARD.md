# Alleystreet Digital Delivery Standard

- **Version:** 1.0
- **Effective:** 2026-09-26
- **Review cadence:** Quarterly and on material change
**Owner:** Alleystreet

This project follows the full standard installed with the `enforce-alleystreet-delivery` skill. Its non-negotiable outcomes are:

1. Written scope, commercial lane, dependencies, acceptance criteria, and approval.
2. Clear client ownership, account control, export, and offboarding.
3. Data minimization, meaningful notice/consent, restricted access, and defined retention/deletion.
4. Least privilege, MFA where supported, safe secret handling, updates, monitoring, and tested recovery.
5. WCAG 2.2 AA target and human testing of critical accessible paths.
6. Failure alerts, bounded retries, duplicate protection, and manual recovery for automations.
7. Human approval for high-impact or irreversible actions; safe, authorized AI use.
8. Transparent payments, recurrence, cancellation, vendor costs, and approved financing providers.
9. Approved facts/assets only; no invented claims, testimonials, guarantees, prices, or certifications.
10. Evidence-backed acceptance tests and a named human release approver.

No project may be labeled ready while a critical gate is unresolved. This standard supports consistent delivery and must be updated as rules and platforms change; it is not a perpetual legal-compliance guarantee.
